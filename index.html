UbiqMap — Radio Tomographic Imaging (interactive simulation)

Radios can see through walls. A moving transmitter–receiver pair measures how much signal each straight path loses; walls absorb more than air. After enough measurements from enough angles, you can solve backwards and recover the floor plan — no cameras, no pre-installed infrastructure. I reproduced the core of “Ubiquitous Indoor Mapping using Mobile Radio Tomography” (Basu, Chakraborty & Jajal, IEEE TMC 2024, SeNSE group, IIT Madras).

Educational reproduction · mirrors the group's own rti-tutorial model

The math (that's all of it)

y = A·x + noise
x̂LS = argmin ‖Ax − y‖²
x̂Tikhonov = argmin ‖Ax − y‖² + λ·‖Lx‖²  (L = Laplacian)
x̂TV = argmin ‖Ax − y‖² + λ·TV(x)  (iteratively reweighted → sharp wall edges)
